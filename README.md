I collaborated with a team of four students to create a GUI and completion of a BudgetBuddy Application using Python and GUI which calculated expenses and savings to guide young individuals with financial literacy and stability in their early career. With over 60+ lines of written code, this experience provided me with skills in team collaboration and communicating effectively to accomplish a unique task. Our goal of creating an innovative design among competitive personalities further suggested we introduce solutions that expanded beyond the classroom, whether it meant working outside of the classroom to discuss ideas that can be implemented in order to meet the requirements of the project. The outcome was evident-- my group received not only full credit on a rigorous evaluation amongst other teams, we were able to go beyond and receive extra points for creativity and overall use of strategies to accomplish the task. 
Designed BudgetBuddy, a personal finance application that records user expenses and income inputs, analyzes spending patterns, and provides real-time feedback on budget status. Implemented logic to evaluate financial thresholds and notify users whether they are within safe spending limits or should increase savings.
